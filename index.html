How to Use the Colour Picker

📁
1. Upload Your Image
Drag and drop or click to browse. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG and AVIF formats up to any size.
🔍
2. Hover to Preview
Move your cursor over the image. A magnifier lens zooms in for pixel-perfect colour selection.
🎯
3. Click to Capture
Click anywhere on the image to instantly capture the colour. HEX, RGB, and HSL values appear immediately.
📋
4. Copy & Use
Copy individual values or all formats at once. Previously picked colours are saved in the history swatches below.

Crafting Tools People Actually Use

RDJ Publishers is an independent web studio dedicated to building free, fast, and privacy-respecting tools for students, professionals, and everyday users – with zero sign-ups and zero costs.

🏢

Who We Are

Independent web publishing studio building practical, free-to-use web apps for everyone. Our tools are lightweight, browser-based, and designed to work on any device.

🎯

Our Mission

Democratise access to productivity tools with browser-based utilities that work on any device, any data plan. No downloads, no installs — just open and use.

✅

Always Free

No hidden paywalls, no freemium limits, no forced sign-ups. All our tools are supported by non-intrusive advertising so they remain completely free to use forever.

🚀

Always Evolving

We regularly add new tools and improve existing ones. We read every feature request and bug report — your feedback directly shapes what we build next.

Your Privacy is Guaranteed

All tools process your data entirely inside your browser. Nothing is ever sent to any server, stored, or seen by anyone else. Your images, text, and files stay private — always.
